Frequently Asked Questions

How does token numbering work in VIZITOR?

Token numbering begins strictly at A-114 for the current active schedule and increments sequentially (A-114, A-115, A-116...) ensuring fair first-come, first-served tracking.

What is Emergency Priority booking?

Emergency and vulnerable patients can be designated with Emergency Priority, which expedites their consultation queue placement directly to the front while preserving standard order for general visits.

Can a single patient book multiple tests or visits?

Yes! While multiple visits create individual queue tokens, VIZITOR's intelligent crowd deduplication ensures each patient is counted as exactly one physical person in the facility crowd density meter.

Where do I view my Digital QR Pass?

Digital QR Passes can be viewed from either the Appointments tab or directly on the Live Queue page via the "View QR Pass" button.
